## Why this agreement

The server and the web site are distributed under AGPL-3.0. The premium module, under a proprietary license, plugs into the server. To distribute this whole, the Project must be able to use each contribution under terms other than the AGPL. Without this agreement, a single outside contribution would be enough to prevent it.
